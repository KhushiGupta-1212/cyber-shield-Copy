⚠️ Warning Signs to Watch For

⏰

Urgent Pressure

Messages demanding immediate action or threatening account suspension may be trying to stop you from thinking carefully.

🎁

Unrealistic Rewards

Unexpected prizes, lottery winnings, or guaranteed rewards can be used to trick people into sharing information or paying fees.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links may lead to fake websites designed to collect login details or financial information.

Remember: This checker looks for selected warning signs. A suspicious result does not prove a scam, and a safe-looking result does not prove a message is genuine. Verify important messages using the organisation's official website or contact details you find independently.
