Cyber Safety Guide

Learn simple and practical ways to stay safe online.

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for your important accounts. Avoid using simple passwords or the same password everywhere.

📱 Account Security

Enable two-factor authentication and keep your account recovery information updated.

🎣 Phishing Protection

Do not click suspicious links or share passwords, OTPs or sensitive information with unknown people.

🌐 Safe Browsing

Use trusted websites, check website addresses carefully and avoid downloading files from unknown sources.
