Essential Cyber Safety Tips

Six important habits to make your digital life safer.

🔐

Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts. Consider a trusted password manager and never reuse your main password everywhere.

ACCOUNT PROTECTION
🔑

Two-Factor Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication wherever available. Never share your OTP, recovery codes or login approval requests with anyone.

EXTRA SECURITY
🎣

Phishing Awareness

Be careful with unexpected messages, urgent warnings and prize offers. Verify the sender independently before opening links or attachments.

SCAM PREVENTION
🌐

Safe Browsing

Check website addresses carefully. HTTPS helps protect the connection, but does not guarantee that a website itself is trustworthy.

BROWSE WISELY
📱

Privacy & Social Media

Limit who can see your posts and personal details. Review app permissions and avoid sharing your location or private information publicly.

PRIVACY FIRST
🔄

Updates & Downloads

Keep your operating system, browser and apps updated. Download software from official sources and avoid unknown files or cracked applications.

Do's & Don'ts Online

Simple choices can help prevent common cyber threats.

✓ Do This

  • Verify suspicious messages using official contact details.
  • Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Report suspicious accounts, messages or transactions.
  • Ask a trusted adult or qualified professional for help when needed.

✕ Avoid This

  • Never share passwords, OTPs or recovery codes.
  • Do not trust links just because a message looks official.
  • Avoid unknown downloads and unverified apps.
  • Do not send money because someone creates panic or urgency.
  • Do not assume a website is safe just because it uses HTTPS.
