Common Cyber Threats

Cyber threats are online activities that can harm your device, accounts, personal information or money. Learn how they work so you can recognize and avoid them.

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick users into revealing passwords, OTPs or personal information.

💳 Online Scams

Fraudulent offers, fake prizes, fake jobs or payment requests designed to steal money or information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices, steal data or allow unauthorized access.

🔑 Identity Theft

When someone misuses another person's personal information for fraudulent activities.

🌐 Fake Websites

Websites created to look legitimate but are designed to collect sensitive information or money.

🎭 Social Engineering

Manipulating people through trust, fear or urgency to make them reveal information or perform an action.
