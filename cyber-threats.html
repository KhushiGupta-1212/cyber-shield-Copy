🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent messages or websites that try to steal passwords, OTPs or personal information.

What is it?

Attackers pretend to be a trusted person or organization to trick people into revealing confidential information.

Example

An unexpected message claims your bank account will be blocked unless you click a link.

How to stay safe

Do not click unexpected links. Contact the organization using its official website or app. Never share an OTP or password.

💳

Online Scams

Fake prizes, job offers, shopping deals and payment requests designed to deceive people.

What is it?

A scam uses deception to obtain money, personal information or access to an account.

Example

A stranger promises a prize but asks you to pay a fee before receiving it.

How to stay safe

Verify offers independently. Do not pay unexpected fees or send money under pressure.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that may damage a device, steal information or enable unauthorized access.

What is it?

Malware is software designed to perform harmful or unauthorized actions.

Example

An untrusted download installs a program that secretly collects information.

How to stay safe

Download apps from trusted sources, update your device and avoid unknown attachments.

🔑

Identity Theft

The misuse of someone's personal details to impersonate them or commit fraud.

What is it?

Someone obtains and misuses another person's identifying information without permission.

Example

A criminal uses stolen personal details to impersonate someone online.

How to stay safe

Share personal documents carefully, use account security features and monitor unexpected account activity.

🌐

Fake Websites

Websites that imitate legitimate services to collect information or payments.

What is it?

A deceptive website may copy a real brand's appearance while using a misleading address.

Example

A fake shopping site advertises unusually cheap products and requests advance payment.

How to stay safe

Check the complete web address, verify the seller and remember that HTTPS alone does not prove a site is legitimate.

🎭

Social Engineering

Manipulating people through trust, fear or urgency to make them disclose information or take action.

What is it?

An attacker exploits human trust or emotions rather than relying only on technical methods.

Example

Someone pretends to be technical support and asks for remote access to your device.

How to stay safe

Pause before acting, verify the person's identity through a trusted channel and never grant unexpected access.
