🚨 What To Do During a Cyber Incident?

Online fraud or a compromised account can be stressful. Stay calm, protect your information, and follow the steps for the situation you are facing.

⚠️ Important: If money has been lost through cyber fraud in India, contact your bank or payment provider immediately and report it through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. You can also call 1930 for financial cyber fraud.
🔐

Account Hacked

Someone may have accessed your email, social media, or another online account.

💳

Online Payment Fraud

You notice an unauthorized payment or suspect a UPI or banking scam.

🎣

Phishing Link Clicked

You opened a suspicious link or entered information on an unfamiliar website.

📱

Phone Lost

Your phone is missing and you need to protect your accounts and personal data.

🪪

Personal Information Misused

Your personal details may have been used without your permission.
