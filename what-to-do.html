🚨 What To Do?

If you face a cyber incident, stay calm and follow the appropriate safety steps below.

🔐 Account Hacked

Change your password immediately and enable two-factor authentication.

💳 Online Payment Fraud

If you notice an unauthorized transaction, contact your bank or payment service immediately.

🎣 Phishing Link Clicked

If you clicked a suspicious link, avoid entering sensitive information and secure your accounts.

📱 Phone Lost

Secure your accounts and contact your mobile service provider if your phone is lost.

🪪 Personal Information Misused

Secure affected accounts and report suspicious activity through appropriate official channels.
